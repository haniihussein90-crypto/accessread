Zaviqu design system

Not a necklace store. A way to say the thing you couldn't.

This page is the single source of truth for how Zaviqu looks, moves, and speaks visually — cinematic, editorial, luxurious, timeless, emotional, minimal. Every screen in the product should be buildable from what's documented here: no new colors, no new fonts, no one-off spacing.

Cinematic

Slow reveals, full-bleed imagery, restraint over noise.

Editorial

Type carries hierarchy — big serif statements, quiet body copy.

Emotional

Copy and layout centre the recipient, not the object.

Minimal

One idea per section. Whitespace is a material, not a leftover.

01 · Foundations

Color

Black and charcoal ground the brand; champagne gold is the only accent — used sparingly, always with meaning (price, CTA, emphasis). White and soft ivory carry the rare light moments. Every text/background pairing below is verified against WCAG AA.

Do

  • Use gold only for accents: eyebrows, CTAs, prices, active states.
  • Use gold-deep (not gold) for gold-colored text on light/ivory sections.
  • Reserve pure white for rare, deliberate high-contrast moments.

Don't

  • Never set body copy in gold — it's an accent, not a text color.
  • Never use gold at full saturation as a large background fill.
  • Never introduce a second accent hue.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond carries every emotional beat — headlines, prices, quotes, signatures. Inter, light and quiet, carries everything functional: navigation, labels, body copy, forms. Never mix the two roles.

Made to order, one at a time

Do

  • Cormorant Garamond for anything emotional: headlines, quotes, prices, gift messages.
  • Inter for anything functional: nav, buttons, labels, body, forms.
  • Uppercase + wide tracking (0.08–0.22em) only on short labels (eyebrows, nav, buttons).

Don't

  • Never set long paragraphs in the display serif — it's for short, weighted lines.
  • Never letter-space body paragraphs; it hurts readability.
  • Never drop below 16px for body copy.

Spacing, Grid & Responsive Rules

Elevation & Shadow

09 · Foundations

Motion

Motion is unhurried — a slow reveal, not a snap. Hover the tiles to feel each timing. Every animation on the site respects prefers-reduced-motion and reduces to an instant state.

fast · 200ms
med · 450ms
slow · 900ms
cinematic · 1400ms

Do

  • Use ease-out for anything entering the viewport (reveal, drawer, modal).
  • Stagger grid/list entrances by ~80ms per item, max 8 items.
  • Animate opacity/transform only — never width, height, or top/left.

Don't

  • Never exceed ~1.5s even for the most "cinematic" entrance.
  • Never animate purely for decoration with no cause-effect relationship.
  • Never block input while an animation plays.
