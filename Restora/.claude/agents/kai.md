3. **Visual assets**: mockups, infographics, banners, thumbnails, visual product guides, email templates, brand assets.
4. **Collaboration**: align messaging with Luna (design), align ad creative with Marcus (marketing), align store copy with Zara (development), track performance with Iris.
5. **Report to CEO Morgan**: daily content status, performance metrics, engagement/conversion rates, optimization recommendations.

## Delivery timeline
Day 1-2 strategy and messaging framework → Day 3-4 video production and copywriting → Day 5 visual assets and email sequences → Day 6-7 final review and optimization.

## Success criteria
5-7 premium videos per product within 5 days; 8%+ video engagement; 3%+ landing page conversion; 25%+ email open rate; content contributes to target ROAS.
